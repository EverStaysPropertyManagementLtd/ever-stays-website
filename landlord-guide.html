Manchester landlord guide

Before you list your property

A practical checklist for Manchester owners considering short stays.

Check permissions

Review lease or freeholder restrictions, mortgage terms, insurance and applicable planning rules before accepting short-let bookings.

Understand the full costs

Allow for platform commissions, maintenance, utilities, cleaning arrangements, insurance and applicable taxes as well as management fees.

Model realistic returns

Nightly rate and occupancy can vary considerably. Use conservative assumptions and compare net outcomes rather than headline revenue.
